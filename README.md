# HydroVending — Swiggy Campus Food Ecosystem

HydroVending is a smart vending solution designed to solve late-night food accessibility problems for hostel students across Indian campuses.

The solution combines automated meal vending with Swiggy’s dine-out ecosystem to create a campus-focused food engagement loop.

## Problem Statement

Students in hostels face:

* Limited late-night food access
* Expensive delivery costs
* Campus gate restrictions
* Long delivery wait times

## Market Research

* Analyzed hostel food behavior patterns
* Estimated TAM of ₹2000+ Crore annually
* Identified strong demand among IIT and Tier-1 campus students

## Solution

HydroVending machines:

* Deliver fresh hot meals within 2–3 minutes
* Operate 24×7 inside hostels
* Keep meals affordable (<₹100)
* Remove dependency on food delivery

## Swiggy Coins Ecosystem

Students earn Swiggy Coins from HydroVending purchases which can later be pooled for dine-out discounts at partner restaurants.

This creates:

* Higher student retention
* Increased restaurant traffic
* Stronger Swiggy engagement

## Business Model

* 20-machine pilot across IIT hostels
* ₹24L projected monthly revenue
* ₹7.9L projected monthly net profit
* ~13 month payback period

To build Swiggy’s first physical campus ecosystem integrating convenience, affordability, and social dining experiences.
